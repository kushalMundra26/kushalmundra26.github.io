Kira: a voice AI agent for live meetings

Kira joins a meeting room, listens to everyone, and answers out loud only when someone says its name. It can search the web and manage a calendar in the middle of a conversation.

  • Real-time pipeline over WebRTC (LiveKit): Deepgram speech-to-text, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, Resemble text-to-speech.
  • Turn-taking with Silero VAD, and the running meeting transcript passed back in as context so answers stay on topic.
  • After launch it replied to every sentence. The wake-word check ran after the request had already reached the LLM, so I moved it in front of the call. That fixed it and cut a lot of wasted API calls.

PersonaGraph: grounding LLMs in a knowledge graph

Can a knowledge graph stop an LLM from contradicting the persona it's meant to play? I built the whole pipeline to find out, from raw government PDFs to the statistics at the end.

9.5 → 71%
persona consistency
500
synthetic personas
5 × 100
models × test cases
  • Pulled statistics out of 4 government PDF reports and generated 500 synthetic personas, checked against the source with chi-square tests (25 of 28 attributes matched).
  • Built a knowledge graph (950 nodes, 6 entity types) and a GraphRAG step that puts each persona's context into the prompt for Qwen 3.5, Gemma 4 and Mistral.
  • Ran the experiments on Warwick's GPU cluster. The gain held for every model (Wilcoxon p < 0.001), and a plain-text baseline did about as well as the graph, so the real win was giving the model the right facts.
